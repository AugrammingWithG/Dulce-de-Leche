# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Mockup: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with Three.js, no build step (user's choice, 15 Oct 2026). Production later: Three.js front end with a Supabase backend (user stated). Shopify and other hosted store builders are ruled out.

## Users

- Primary (inferred from the cafe's Instagram, to confirm with the client): people in Narellan and the wider Macarthur region of south-west Sydney. Families planning birthdays, christenings and weddings who need a custom cake; early-morning customers, since the cafe opens at 5am; and the Latin American community in Macarthur.
- Immediate audience for this mockup: the cafe owners, who see it in a meeting on 15 Oct 2026 and decide whether to hire the agency (Cre8tive Sync).

## Product Purpose

The website for Dulce de Leche Cafe, a Uruguayan cafe and celebration-cake maker opening at Shop 3/25 Kirkham St, Narellan on Tuesday 20 October 2026, open 5am–5pm. It brings people through the door (hours, location, menu), takes custom cake orders properly (occasion, size, flavour, design, date, deposit) instead of Instagram DMs, and lets people order pastries and coffee ahead for pickup. Success for the mockup: the owners say yes.

## Positioning

"Authentic Uruguayan treats": Uruguayan specialities (dulce de leche, milhoja cake, chivito) and custom celebration cakes with sugar-flower work, from a cafe whose stated mission is "to celebrate and grow Latin American culture in Macarthur and beyond".

## Operating Context

- Brand-new shopfront opening 20 Oct 2026. History on Instagram (@dulcedelechecafe2024) with cakes, pastries and menu posts; a new account @dulcedelechecafenarellan (209 followers, 3 posts at 15 Oct 2026); a Facebook group. Has taken part in the Macarthur Latin Food Market.
- How cake orders come in today is unconfirmed (assumed: Instagram DMs and phone).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed products (from Instagram): celebration cakes (wedding tiers with sugar flowers, birthdays, christenings, photo-print cakes, milhoja, Continental cakes), filled croissants, loaded muffins (Kit Kat, berry, peach), Biscoff treats and cheesecake, chivito ($20, add chips $3), coffee.
- Undecided: online ordering scope, deposits, lead times, delivery, payment provider and till system, domain (dulcedelechecafe.com.au is registered; owner unconfirmed).
- The client will send logo files and the menu with prices later. No real photos are on hand yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Dulce de Leche Cafe.
- Existing identity: chocolate-brown ground; cream script wordmark "Dulce de Leche" with a steaming-cup mark and "CAFE" in serif capitals; script "Grand Opening" and serif capitals on posters.
- Uruguayan identity: "Authentic Uruguayan treats" and the Uruguayan flag in their bio.

## Evidence on Hand

- Instagram screenshots shared by the user: opening date, hours, address, mission quote, "Chivito $20 / Add chips $3", product photos (screenshots only, not usable files).
- Not on hand: logo files, original photos, full menu and prices, reviews or testimonials. Never fabricate testimonials, ratings, prices or awards; label any illustrative content as a placeholder.

## Product Principles

1. The cakes are the proof: show the craft (sugar flowers, milhoja, tiers) first.
2. Visiting is effortless: hours, address and map always one tap away.
3. Replace DM threads with an order flow that captures everything the bakers need.
4. Uruguayan pride and community, never generic "artisan bakery".
5. Rich 3D that still loads fast on a phone.
