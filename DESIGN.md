# Design System: Dulce de Leche Cafe

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The 5am Night Bake"**

The customer site is a dark cocoa room at five in the morning where the only lit things are the treats. There is no full-bleed photo over a cream grid of product cards. The ground is cocoa-black (ground to night), lit by one warm key light from the upper left with a caramel rim behind the product. The type is steamed-milk cream, the counters and chapter numbers are polished brass, and the progress rules are thin. The page opens on one alfajor in live 3D, and the scroll takes it apart in four numbered chapters: **Whole**, **Break** (the halves part and strands of dulce de leche stretch until they snap), **Inside** (the layers lift apart, each labelled with a brass leader line), and **Fresh** (it lands back together on a lit wood counter).

Everything after the story stays in the room. The favourites are unboxed treats sitting straight on the cocoa ground in a pool of caramel light. Each product opens on a turntable. All ordering goes into one drawer that ends in a scalloped card-stock pickup ticket. The full menu is a book whose pages turn on the spine. Paper shows up only where a real shop would hand you paper: the form slips, the ticket, the book pages, the lit cream arches of the cake section and the printed catalogue. Density is generous: 132px between sections, a 1240px column inside a fluid 20 to 80px gutter, and large Gloock headlines set tight.

Motion follows one grammar, **lift and settle**, on one curve (`cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)`). The story chapters are the exception. They are scrubbed by scroll position, not triggered by it, so the visitor sets the pace.

Behind the counter, the staff "counter view" (`admin.html`) runs in an **Operate register** that this redesign did not touch. It keeps the older counter-wood and card-stock furniture (wood top bar, card-stock shelves on brass rails, caramel for action, brass for trim) at working density. No 3D, no reveals, and no script beyond the wordmark. Its one move is the tick: when an order lands, the changed cells flash brass and roll their numbers.

**Key Characteristics:**
- A cocoa ground that never goes black: the darkest any pixel gets, in CSS or in the 3D finish, is ground (#140A06).
- The treats are the light source of the page: live or studio-rendered 3D under a warm key, with a caramel rim or a caramel pool beneath.
- A four-chapter, scroll-scrubbed product story with brass chapter numbers on thin progress rules.
- Paper appears only as a real object: slips, the scalloped ticket, book pages, lit arches, the printed catalogue.
- One order, reachable from anywhere: hero, favourites, product sheet, Menu Book and the business section all add to the same drawer.
- Honest placeholders: anything the cafe hasn't supplied is labelled, and owner-facing notes hide behind a Pitch notes switch.
- Two registers sharing one store: the night-bake showroom for customers, the wood-and-card-stock counter view for staff.

## Colors

A low-key cocoa palette lit by steamed-milk type, polished brass and one caramel action colour. The only light surfaces are paper objects.

### Primary
- **Dulce de Leche Caramel** (caramel): the action colour. It fills the primary buttons ("Order alfajores", "Add N to order", "Pay and place order", "Next", "Get directions", "View order"), the order count once the order has something in it, and the hover state of every Add button. It is the ring of a live quantity stepper, the current builder step bar, the Pitch notes switch when on, the cover rule in the Menu Book, text selection, the caret, and the 3D cake ribbon. In the counter view it fills the next-step buttons and today's sales bars. **Toasted Caramel** (caramel-hi) is its hover. **Burnt Caramel** (caramel-deep) is the text-weight version on paper: the script on the book and catalogue covers, the form confirmation, pitch notes on paper, the scrollbar thumb, and the lower edge of the counter view's brass rail.

### Secondary
- **Polished Brass** (brass-hi): lettering and trim on the dark ground. It carries the script wordmark, the chapter numbers and the progress fill of the story rail, the layer-label dots and leader lines, the nav underline, the focus ring, the Visit table labels, the tray hint, the current Menu Book dot, the selected tab and chip text, and the pitch notes on dark. In the counter view it carries order times, group labels and shelf titles.
- **Case Brass** (brass): the brass tint. At 22% it fills a selected tab, flavour chip or segment on dark. At 16% it marks a selected platter card and the counter view's order tags. At 12% it washes the "usual" and chips offers in the drawer. At 55% it is the counter view's day-before bar.
- **Brass Flash** (flash): counter view only, the flash on a stock cell whose count just changed.

### Tertiary
- **Working states** (counter view only, always on card stock or cream). **Oxblood** (danger) is the error and Cancel order colour; its ring is danger-line, and it warms to danger-bg on hover. **Herb Green** (success) marks the "more" delta, and **Sage Dot** (success-dot) marks a ready order. **Burnt Toffee** (warn) on **Toffee Wash** (warn-bg) is the Running low flag.

### Neutral
- **5am Ground** (ground): the page, the story stage, the favourites, the Who's ordering, roots and visit sections, the solid nav (92%) and the phone action bar (96%). It is also the floor the 3D finish never drops below.
- **5am Night** (night): the middle of the favourites and visit gradients, the counter-view page, and text on caramel and polished brass.
- **Cocoa** (cocoa): the raised dark surfaces. It is the order drawer, the product sheet's info side, the map's empty state, and the Pitch notes switch.
- **Counter Wood** (wood): the 3D stages (the cake builder, the drawer's tray) inside radial vignettes. In the counter view it is the top bar, the orders rail and the phone New order bar.
- **Chocolate** (choc) and **Milk Chocolate** (choc-2, the hover): the Order pill, the ink button, every selected control on paper (choice chips, slots, days, segments, calendar days), the booked note, the toast, and the counter view's Sold out flag.
- **Card Stock** (card): slips, inputs, the pickup ticket, choice chips, and the hot centre of the arches and the business platter stage. **Warm Card Stock** (card-warm) is the Menu Book page. **Tissue Paper** (paper) is the calendar day and the placed-order note. **Doily Cream** (cream) is the outer ring of the platter stage and the counter view's drawers.
- **Arch Glow** (arch-mid) and **Arch Edge** (arch-edge): the outer stops of the lit radial inside each cake arch.
- **Cocoa Ink** (ink) and **Soft Cocoa Ink** (ink-soft): text on paper. Soft ink carries descriptions, allergen lines, folios and hints.
- **Steamed Milk** (on-dark) and **Latte Foam** (on-dark-soft): text on the dark ground. Latte foam carries ledes, descriptions and captions.
- **Paper Rule** (line), **Pressed Edge** (line-strong) and **Brass Haze** (line-dark): hairlines on paper, control borders on paper, and every hairline and ring on the dark ground.

### Named Rules
**The Cocoa Floor Rule.** The room is cocoa, never black. Page grounds start at ground (#140A06), and the 3D finish clamps every pixel to at least the same value, so the canvas and the page meet without a seam.

**The One Action Rule.** Caramel means "do this" or "this is on". No other hue fills an action button except chocolate (the ink button and the Order pill), and in the counter view caramel also means today against brass for the day before.

**The Brass Selection Rule.** On the dark ground, a chosen thing takes a brass tint: 16 to 22% case brass, a polished-brass ring at 50 to 60%, and polished-brass text. On paper it fills chocolate. Caramel stays free to mean the action.

**The Lit Paper Rule.** Light surfaces are never pure white on screen. They are card stock, warm card or tissue paper, and they appear only as objects (slip, ticket, page, arch, stage), never as a section band. Only the printed catalogue sits on white paper.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Gloock (with Times New Roman, serif)
**Body Font:** Figtree (with system-ui, sans-serif)
**Script Font:** Yesteryear (with cursive)

**Character:** Gloock is a high-contrast, sign-painted serif. Set large and tight (-0.02 to -0.03em) in cream on cocoa, it reads like lettering lit from the counter. Figtree keeps everything else plain and friendly. Yesteryear is the cafe's name and nothing else.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Gloock 400, clamp(2.7rem, 5.2vw, 5.3rem), 0.97, -0.03em): the hero H1 only, max 11ch; 2.6rem at 760px and below.
- **Chapter** (Gloock 400, clamp(2.4rem, 4.6vw, 4.4rem), 0.98, -0.03em): chapters two to four of the story, max 10ch; 2.4rem on phones.
- **Headline** (Gloock 400, clamp(2.3rem, 4.6vw, 4.1rem), 1.0, -0.02em): section H2s, balanced wrapping, revealed with the lift-title clip.
- **Sheet title** (Gloock 400, clamp(2.2rem, 3.6vw, 3.2rem)): the product sheet's name.
- **Page title** (Gloock 400, clamp(1.7rem, 2.6vw, 2.5rem), 1.0): Menu Book page headings.
- **Title large** (Gloock 400, 1.8 to 1.9rem, about 1.05 to 1.1): offer rows, slip headings, builder step headings, the drawer title.
- **Favourite name** (Gloock 400, clamp(1.6rem, 2.2vw, 2rem), 1.05).
- **Layer label** (Gloock 400, 1.35rem, 1.1; .95rem on phones): the labels in chapter three.
- **Title** (Gloock 400, 1.15 to 1.3rem): item names in the drawer, the book and the arches; business list items; platter cards; calendar months.
- **Price** (Gloock 400, 1.3rem): a real price. When there is no price, Figtree .84rem latte foam reads "From your menu".
- **Body** (Figtree 400, 17px, 1.6; 16px at 760px and below). **Lede** (1.08rem, latte foam, max 56ch). Chapter copy is capped at 36ch.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from light, not stacked surfaces. The dark sections are flat, and the treats are lit, so depth lives in the 3D: soft shadow maps under a warm key, a caramel rim, and a photographic finish. Paper objects cast long, soft, tucked-under shadows with large negative spread, like objects under a single overhead light. Dialogs cast deeper versions of the same shadow off the edge they enter from.

### The photographic grade (story stage)
The story alfajor is graded like a photograph:
- **Tone:** ACES filmic tone mapping at 1.05.
- **Reflections:** a Poly Haven studio HDR at .3 (the generated RoomEnvironment at .32 when opened from file://).
- **Lights:** a warm hemisphere fill at .22, a 2.5 key from the upper left with soft VSM shadows, a caramel (#FFB566) rim point light behind right, and a spotlight pool that comes up for the counter chapter.
- **Ambient occlusion:** GTAO, shifted cool, so crevices lose red faster than blue, like bounced skylight.
- **Finish pass:** a light bloom (.16), saturation at 1.12, shadows cooled by up to 30% below mid luminance, a 30% vignette, a 0.1% radial lens fringe, film grain at .022, and the cocoa floor clamp.
- **On phones (760px and below):** no AO, no lens fringe, grain at .008, so the coconut doesn't turn to speckle.

The product sheet, builder, tray, platter and studio renders share ACES at 1.05, the same warm key from the upper left, and a caramel rim. They leave out the post finish.

### Named Rules
**The One Key Light Rule.** Light comes from one warm key at the upper left, with a caramel rim or pool from behind or below. Shadows are soft and tucked under their object, or cast off a dialog's leading edge. Never a hard offset, and never a coloured glow on a UI control. The caramel light under a treat is lighting the product and is allowed.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the customer site on the cocoa ground (#140A06 to #1C0F09), and let the treats be the light: one warm key from the upper left, a caramel rim or pool.
- **Do** bring paper in only as an object: a card-stock slip, the scalloped ticket, a book page, a lit arch, a platter stage.
